An adventure awaits you in Berlin

📋 Instructions

How It Works:

  • Follow the clues: You'll receive a series of riddles and clues that will guide you to different locations around Berlin.
  • Answer each clue: Type your answer in the text box. Don't worry if you get stuck — after 5 wrong attempts, the answer will be revealed!
  • Choose your adventure: After completing the main clues, you'll get to choose between two different paths for your final challenge. This task doesn't have to be completed the same day.
  • Take your time: There's no rush! Enjoy each location and experience along the way. It's meant to take all weekday, but you can break it into two days as the clues indicate.

💡 Tips:

  • Answers are case insensitive — no need to worry about capitals!
  • Use the app you've worked so hard on all these years to find your answers.
  • Read each clue carefully — the details matter!
  • Bring your phone charged, good walking shoes, and a pen. Cost is covered via venmo.
  • Don't forget to capture your journey in the journal you'll pick up along the way!
